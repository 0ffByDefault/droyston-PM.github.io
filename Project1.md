# Veteran Services Culture Rebrand  
**University of Nevada, Reno**

---

## Situation
Despite increased participation in Veteran Services programming, key demographics—including female veterans, veterans of color, and veterans with children—remained underrepresented.

---

## Tools
Surveys · Focus Groups · Stakeholder Engagement · Data Analysis · Event Planning

---

## Actions
- Designed and deployed engagement surveys  
- Facilitated focus groups  
- Analyzed participation data  
- Developed and executed targeted engagement strategy  

---

## Results
Increased participation among underrepresented groups and improved awareness of services.
