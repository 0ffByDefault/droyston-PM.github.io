# Social Security Disability Insurance Outreach Access and Response (SOAR) Process Tailoring  
**Telecare Corporation (HART)**

---

## Situation
As a Peer Recovery Coach at Telecare’s HART, I was tasked by the HART Program Manager with learning the SOAR process and tailoring a set of standards operating procedures to the specific needs of the Housing Assistance and Retention Team.

---

## Tools
Smartsheet, Stakeholder Identification & Engagement, Collaborative Meetings, Resource Management

---

## Actions
- Leveraged available knowledge base to become the the program’s internal subject matter expert  
- Developed an engagement strategy for external stakeholders across all supportive housing sites  
- Tailored SOAR learning curriculum to HART program operations and clientele  
- Created tailored work breakdown structure and schedule baseline for process implementation  
- Briefed program manager and team members on SOAR methodology and tailored implementation plans to receive feedback and continuously improve operations  

---

## Results
Telecare HART implemented the SOAR process for clients at supportive housing sites across Santa Barbara county increasing access to assistance and accurate information regarding Social Security Disability Insurance applications.
