# Microsoft Co-Sell Handoff  
**Affirma**

---

## Situation
While serving as a Support Analyst at Affirma, I was tasked with developing and implementing a transition plan for the handoff of the Microsoft Co-Sell program to another vendor to ensure continuity and program compliance.

---

## Tools
Azure Cloud Database, Microsoft Suite (Excel, Outlook, Teams), Staff Training, Stakeholder Identification Analysis and Engagement, Knowledge Transfer

---

## Actions
- Developed and presented transition plan to Affirma Business Process Outsourcing Director  
- Provided regular updates to BPO director, lead support analyst, and Co-Sell program management  
- Identified and engaged relevant vendor staff for knowledge transfer  
- Facilitated and led knowledge transfer sessions between Affirma Deal Review and Validation teams and incoming vendor staff  
- Assisted reduced staff in maintaining SLA compliance during transition period  

---

## Results
Successfully managed the handoff of the Microsoft Co-Sell program while maintaining all contract Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
